---
title: "The deck grows by its own draws"
date: "2026-10-28"
summary: "From today a new card enters the deck only when The Sower or The Miner is the morning's card."
---

From 28 October on, the deck grows only when two of its own cards call for it. When
[The Sower](/cards/the-sower/) is the morning's card, one new card grows from a seed that comes from
outside the deck: something from the day, a random page of a book, a sentence someone else said. When
[The Miner](/cards/the-miner/) is the morning's card, one new card is dug from a gap between two
cards: a meaning the deck circles but no card holds.

Only the morning's card counts, upright or reversed; the lens card never does. Chance decides when:
two new cards may come in one week, or none for months.

Each new card is announced here, and its page names the morning that called for it. The rule is on
the [about page](/about/#draw).
