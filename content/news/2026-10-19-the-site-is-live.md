---

Sower Tarot has a home of its own. The site shows every card of the deck, 37 today, each with its
picture, its moving clip and its description, and every morning at seven it draws one card and posts
a reading for the day. The [archive](/readings/) begins with the reading of 13 October.

The morning's card is picked by a public random number, so anyone can check that no hand chose it.
Each reading names the number it came from; the [about page](/about/#draw) explains how to check a
draw.

The site, its cards and texts, and the skills that make new cards and the daily reading are open
source at [github.com/rayh1/sowertarot](https://github.com/rayh1/sowertarot).
