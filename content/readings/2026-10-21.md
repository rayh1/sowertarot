## The card

An old oak stands against a deep blue twilight sky. Its branches are bare. Not a leaf, not a bud. Seen from above the ground, it could pass for a dead tree.

The card shows what the eye would miss. Below the cut-away ground line the roots spread as wide as the crown, and one thick taproot glows gold all the way down to the bedrock. A young woman kneels by the trunk with her hand held out. The only warm light in the scene comes from under the earth.

## Essence

The Root is the hidden foundation that every visible growth depends on. It is slow, plain work: holding on, drawing water, spreading through the dark. Reversed, that work is not missing. It is being doubted. You look at the bare branches, see nothing, and decide the tree has stopped. The temptation is to dig the roots up to check on them, or to force a leaf out before its time.

## For today

Find the part of your life that looks like those branches. A project that has gone quiet. A change you started that shows no result yet. A recovery, a habit, a slow repair between you and someone. What would you see if you could cut the ground away beneath it?

Today, leave it bare. Do not measure it, do not report on it, do not pull it up to look. Do the small, dull thing that feeds it, the watering kind of work, and then walk away from it. The oak does not grow leaves in the evening to prove it is alive.

If you feel the itch to check, put your hand out like the woman does, without grabbing. Notice what is still holding you up. That is enough proof for one day.
