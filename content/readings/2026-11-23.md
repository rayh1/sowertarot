## The card

A young man in a long purple cloak stands just inside an old stone arch. In his hand is a large iron key. He has not put it away, and he has not used it either. He simply holds it.

Around him the scene is already moving. A black cat walks out through the doorway at his feet. A raven sits on a branch inside the arch. Beyond, a pale path winds into a misty forest, and the dawn light is slowly growing at its far end.

## Essence

The Threshold is the pause between what was and what will be. Upright, it says that this pause has power of its own. You are not stuck at the edge. You are standing where the choice still lives, holding the means to make it, while the way ahead shows itself step by step.

## For today

Look at what you are holding. It may be a skill you have not used in a while, an offer you have not answered, or a plan you keep in a drawer. It is not lost, and it is not yet spent. What would keep it alive until you are ready to turn it?

Give it some small care today. Take it out and handle it: open the old notes, reply to say you are still thinking, practise the skill for one evening. Do not force the door open, and do not leave the key on a ledge to rust.

The cat has already gone ahead. Notice where your instinct is walking, and let that tell you when the time has come to step after it.
