## The card

Out on the dark water, a tall sailing ship holds its course toward a lighthouse. The sea is restless and the clouds drift across the night, but the ship keeps its heading. High on the tower, a keeper in a dark blue coat stands beside the great lamp, whose broad beams sweep the sky.

The keeper will never meet the people on that ship. The ship will never stop at the tower. The light is not where the ship is going. It is what lets the ship keep going through the dark.

## Essence

The Lighthouse Keeper stands for purposeful solitude: a life given meaning by being steadily useful to strangers. It honours work that no one sees and a watch that holds through every hour. Upright, the card shows that service and safe passage work as a pair. One side keeps the light steady; the other side trusts it and moves.

## For today

Today, look at the ship. A ship at night does not wait for the coast to be clear before it sails. It moves with what it can see: one beam, one bearing, one stretch of water at a time. What light are you steering by right now, and are you actually moving toward it?

Pick one thing you have been circling and take the next leg of it today. Not the whole crossing, just the part you can see. Check your bearing against the light, correct a little, and keep going. The ship learns the sea by sailing it.

If you are the one keeping a light for others today, that matters too. But give yourself the ship's part as well: a course of your own, sailed with care, one swell at a time.
