## The card

A figure stands between deep furrows, back to us, one arm swept wide. Golden seed hangs in the air in a long arc. The sun is pale and large, the air hazy, the soil dark and freshly turned.

Above the field, birds wheel. They are chance in the picture: some of that seed will feed them, not the ground. The sower does not look up at them. Feet together, the figure stays where it stands and keeps casting.

## Essence

The Sower is effort given to a future you cannot control. Seed must leave the hand before anything can grow, and not every seed takes. Reversed, that release stalls. The hand stays half closed, or the sower stops mid-field to count losses, eyes on the birds, ready to chase them off. The work turns into guarding, and guarding plants nothing.

## For today

Think of something you started and then began to hover over: a message sent, a plan shared, a piece of work handed on. Ask yourself: what are the birds actually taking, and what am I only afraid they will take?

Spend a few quiet minutes looking at it as it is. Note what has landed, what has gone, and what is still in the air. Do not fix, chase or explain anything while you look. Seeing the loss at its real size is often enough to free the hand for the next cast.
