## The card

A dirt road splits around a tall grey stone and joins again in a golden meadow. Three travellers have met there: an old shepherd with a wooden staff, a young woman in a saffron cloak, and a fisherman holding a basket of bread. Their hands reach toward the stone.

Behind them the single road keeps going. It runs across the meadow toward a thin line of sea, far off, under a quiet cream sky. The meeting happens here, but the road was never meant to end at the stone.

## Essence

The Convergence is the moment when separate streams of a life, or separate lives, come together and show a pattern none of them showed alone. It is integration: keeping many paths instead of choosing one, and letting what you know, who you know and what pulls against itself inform each other.

## For today

Notice where two things in your life have recently met: two people, two projects, an old skill and a new problem. The meeting itself can feel like the arrival. The card says otherwise. The road carries on past the stone, and now it is one road.

The question for today is where that shared road is heading. Not its end point, which no one can name yet, but its direction. Say that direction out loud, to the other person or on paper for yourself: "we are heading toward this."

Then walk the first stretch side by side. Plan the next meeting, put both names on the same page, open the shared folder. Let the joined path have somewhere to go, and keep it pointed there even if the sea stays far away.
