## The card

Through the gap in the violet curtain lies a lake. It is still, rippled only with silver, and it holds a black sky full of stars. Low golden hills line the far shore, and a coral-red hill rises between them on the horizon.

A woman in pale blue stands on the near side. She has drawn the curtain aside with one hand; the other hangs at her side. The sandy path at her bare feet runs to the water and stops there.

## Essence

The Veil is the boundary between the known and the unknown. It spans the whole scene, so there is no way around it, only through. Some truths show themselves only to someone who comes with patience and humility. The card holds two choices open: part the curtain and step into new understanding, or respect the shelter that not knowing gives. Reversed, the hand is on the curtain but the looking stalls. The pause at the edge stretches into a habit, and the mystery beyond is kept at arm's length instead of honoured.

## For today

The lake reflects the stars only because nothing disturbs it. Reversed, the disturbance comes from your side of the curtain. You have opened a gap on something, a question about your work, a person, or yourself, and then kept busy at the edge: weighing, rehearsing, arguing with what might be there. All that motion ripples the water, and the picture it gives back is blurred.

So what would the water show if you stopped stirring it?

Stand at the gap and keep still. Write down, in one plain sentence, what lies on the other side as far as you honestly know it now, and leave the sentence as it is. It is not a decision and not a plan, only the reflection without distortion. Whether you step through or let the curtain fall can wait until that sentence is clear.
