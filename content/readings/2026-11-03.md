## The card

A woman in blue stands on a low ledge deep in a canyon. Her hands are at her lips, the call already made. Red, ochre and violet walls rise on either side, and swallows cut back and forth between them.

Just below her feet, a green river runs the length of the canyon. It flows away from her, toward a small sun setting at the far end, and it takes with it whatever reaches the water. She stands still. The river does not.

## Essence

The Echo is the law of call and response. What you send into the world comes back to you, shaped by everything it passed through on the way. Reversed, the card points to the stretch after the call, where the urge to manage the answer grows larger than the call itself. You keep standing guard over words that have already moved downstream.

## For today

Follow the river with your eyes. It carries her call to places she will never stand: round bends, past walls, into light she cannot reach. Nothing in the picture suggests she should run along the bank. So where are you still trying to steer something that has already left your hands? Perhaps it is a decision you explained, a piece of work you handed in, or a hard thing you finally said to someone.

Today, put down the job of minding how it lands. Skip the follow-up that only restates your point. Leave the explanation as it stands, without a second version to soften or sharpen it. If the urge to check comes up, picture the green water rounding the next bend without you.

The answer will come back in its own time and its own form. Until it does, you are free to walk away from the ledge.
