## The card

A young traveller stands on a rocky summit in a long grey cloak, an open brass compass on his palm. Above him the twilight sky has turned deep teal-green, scattered with small stars. One of them burns brighter than all the rest. Below, a river winds between ridges toward the sea, and gold light lies along the horizon.

Reversed, the eye goes up and stays there. The traveller holds the compass, yet he steers by the bright star without asking how it came to be the one he follows.

## Essence

The Compass Rose is the sense of direction we carry inside: the faculty that finds the way when the map runs out. Upright, the needle and the star agree, and the traveller chooses with a clear head. Reversed, that guidance is crowded out. You follow a fixed point out of habit, and its brightness passes for proof that it is right. The faculty is not lost. It is waiting to be consulted.

## For today

What is the bright star you steer by right now? It might be a goal, someone's approval, a rule about who you should be. Did you pick it, or was it simply the brightest thing in the sky when you first looked up?

Take a decision you face today and say out loud, or write down, why you are making it this way. Listen to the reason. If it is "because that is what I do" or "because they expect it", you have found a star you inherited.

You may keep it. Many inherited stars are good ones. But before you move, look down at the needle in your own hand and see whether it points the same way. Then take the step by the heading you chose.
