## The card

Look closely at the ribbon of birds and pick out one starling. It has no map of the spiral it is drawing. It only watches the few birds right beside it and turns when they turn. Thousands of those small turns make the great curl across the winter sky.

Below, pale gold reeds run out to dark trees and low hills. A narrow stream winds up through the marsh toward you. There is no one watching in the scene. The place to stand is yours.

## Essence

The Murmuration is the many moving as one, with no leader. Each bird is itself and also part of a shape it cannot see whole. Reversed, that balance tips. The single bird turns so fast with its neighbours that it never asks where the flock is going. Or it holds itself so far apart that it loses the shape altogether. Either way, belonging has stopped being a choice and become a reflex.

## For today

Which few people do you take your turns from? A colleague whose mood sets yours, a friend whose opinions you repeat, a feed you scroll before you decide anything. You did not sit down and pick them. They are simply the birds that happened to be nearest.

Today, pick one on purpose. Choose a person whose direction you would like to share, and go and fly beside them for a while: a call, a coffee, a shared task. Let their turns reach you instead of the ones that just happen to be close.

Notice how the day bends when your nearest bird is someone you chose. The flock is still the flock. Your place in it is something you can set.
