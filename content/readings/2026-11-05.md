## The card

A single ember glows in a bed of white ash inside a plain clay bowl. A figure sits close, face in shadow, one hand on a knee and the other held open toward the bowl, near enough to feel the heat. Smoke climbs in a thin, even line. It neither thins nor thickens.

On the stone floor lie a few sticks of fresh kindling. Nobody has touched them. The ash is what has already burned; the sticks are what could burn next. Between them sits the hand, warm and waiting.

## Essence

The Ember is potential at its smallest and most fragile: a fire that is neither out nor alive. It asks for care, and then for a choice. Some coals deserve shelter, some deserve fuel, and some deserve to be let go cold. The ember stage has its own strength, but it is a stage, not a home.

## For today

Reversed, the card shows care that has turned into stalling. The hand stays open over the bowl, keeping the glow safe, while the sticks lie a few inches away. Sheltering feels responsible. Done for too long, it becomes a way to avoid the risk of feeding the fire and watching it fail to catch.

What small thing have you kept warm for weeks without ever adding to it? A half-written letter, a plan you mention but never start, a friendship you think about more than you call. Name it plainly.

Then lay one stick on it today. Send the message, write the first page, book the hour. Once it leaves your hand you cannot take it back, and it may not catch. A coal that is never fed goes grey anyway.
