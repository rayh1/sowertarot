## The card

A woman stands barefoot on a flat rock at the edge of a still lake. Her hands are pressed together at her chest, and her face is lifted toward a pale morning sky where an orange crescent moon is fading. Reeds grow at the water's edge, and blue wooded hills line the far shore.

Her hands are the centre of the picture. They are not reaching for anything or holding anything up for others. They rest on her own chest, where the breath rises and falls, as if she is checking that she is here.

## Essence

The Breath is the plainest act of being alive. It happens thousands of times a day without a thought, and each time it can bring you back to the present. It is the bridge between inner and outer, the rhythm under everything else. Drawn upright, it means that peace is never far away: it is always one breath off.

## For today

Notice her hands. Yours will be busy all day: typing, carrying, waving, holding a phone, shaking someone's hand. Each of those belongs to a part you play, the colleague, the parent, the friend, the customer at the counter. All of them are honest. The breath under them stays the same in every one. It is the part of you that is never anyone in particular.

When you move from one part of your day to the next, who is breathing in between?

Make the change-overs your practice today. As you leave a meeting, end a call or come home, pause before you go on. Put both hands flat on your chest and feel one full breath go in and out. Let it belong to no one: not the worker, not the helper, not the one people expect. Then lower your hands and go on as whoever the next hour needs. The breath comes with you.
