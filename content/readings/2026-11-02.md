## The card

A young woman in a long sky-blue cloak stands on a clifftop at sunset. She is half turned away, looking out over a calm golden sea. Pale boulders lie in the amber grass at her feet, and a rocky outcrop rises on the right.

At the centre of everything sits a round orange sun, set exactly on the flat line where sea meets sky. It has not gone yet. Its light still spreads across the whole sea and up into the violet sky, where four birds fly high and the first evening star has come out.

## Essence

The Horizon is the edge between what you can see and what you cannot. It moves as you move, so you never reach it. That is not failure; it is what keeps you growing. The card is about aspiration as a direction rather than a place: you are drawn forward by the reaching itself, and the edge widens as you do.

## For today

Look at the sun on that line. In a few minutes it will be gone from her view, yet the light it is giving right now still reaches the water, the grass and her face. It spends itself freely on a shore it will not stay to see, and it comes back in the morning from the other side.

So the question for today is this: what can you put out toward your own far edge before the light changes? Something real and finished enough to leave your hands. A letter sent, a draft shared, an hour given to the long project, an offer made to someone who may or may not answer.

Do it once and let it go. Some of it will come to nothing, and that is part of the cost. What you want to see later can only come from what you give away now, and you will be standing somewhere else by the time it shows.
