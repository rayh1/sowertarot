## The card

A pilgrim in an orange travelling coat walks away from us, leaning on a stick. The dirt road ahead winds back and forth across green foothills, marked here and there by small stone cairns.

Far off, where the road ends, a small golden chapel stands at the foot of purple mountains, under a line of white birds. At this distance it is tiny. Yet it catches the dawn light, and the eye goes to it before anything else in the picture.

## Essence

The Pilgrim is the seeker who has chosen the long way on purpose. The road changes the one who walks it; the chapel draws him on. Pilgrimage here is a practice: discipline, sacrifice, and full attention to each step, in answer to a call that others may not hear. Upright, the card shows that resolve in good order. The goal is clear, the walker is moving, and the length of the road is accepted.

## For today

Many bends of this road turn the pilgrim away from the chapel for a while. He walks east to go north. What keeps him on course is that he still knows where the chapel stands.

What is your chapel right now? Write it down in a single plain sentence: the thing your long effort is for. Keep it short enough to fit on a scrap of paper.

Then, when a choice comes up today, hold it against that sentence. A side road, a request, a new task: does it bring the chapel closer, even by a winding route, or does it lead off toward some other place? Some bends that look like detours are part of the road. Walk the ones that belong to it, and let the others pass.
