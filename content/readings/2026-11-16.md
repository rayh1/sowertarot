## The card

A woman in a long dark-green gown walks a pale stone path through a night garden. She holds up a tall silver candelabra, and its five candles burn warm against the stars. White roses crowd both sides of the path, and bare trees frame the sky.

Behind her, the same path runs back to a small stone chapel with a green door and a cross on its roof. That is where the flames were lit. She has carried them out, but the way back is still there, pale in the candlelight.

## Essence

The Candelabra is light carried into the dark: many flames rising from one stem, many ways of understanding held in a single hand. It stands for knowledge and faith that grow by being shared, and for the long human work of lighting the dark, alone and together. Upright, the light is lit, held high and on the move.

## For today

Notice the path behind her. It leads back to the chapel door. A candle carried far enough burns low, and the place to light it again is where it was first lit. Going back is not losing ground. Each time she returns and sets out again, she knows the garden better and walks a little further into it.

What did you learn long ago that you have stopped going back to? Today, return to it. Reread the book, the letter or the old notes. Call the person who first showed you how. Go to the place where the idea first made sense.

Then come out again with it. You are not the person who learned it the first time, so it will show you something new. Carry that fresh flame into the darkest part of your day, and walk a few steps beyond where you stopped before.
