## The card

A woman sits still on a stone dais between two tall columns wound with golden ivy. Her eyes are closed. A glowing crystal orb rests in her clasped hands, and beneath them, across her knees, lies a rolled scroll. Its writing is in an ancient hand that no one reads anymore.

She is not trying to decipher it. The scroll rests on her like something handed down, set there by people who held this seat long before her. Above her, between the columns, the first few stars come out.

## Essence

The Oracle stands for inner knowing: truth reached through stillness rather than effort. She does not predict anything. She gives back to the seeker what the seeker has forgotten. The answers are already present, and the work is to grow quiet enough to hear them. The scroll adds that this knowing is older than any single life. It reaches her as presence, not as words she could parse.

## For today

A lot of what you know did not start with you. A way of calming a tense room, a sense of when to speak and when to wait, a habit of checking twice: someone near you showed it, and someone showed them. Like the scroll, it is written in a hand you could never trace back to its first line.

So today's question is this: what do you carry that came from the few people you move alongside every day?

Sit quietly for a few minutes and picture those people, one by one. For each, find a piece of knowing that passed to you without anyone teaching it. Then, in your next conversation with one of them, stay quiet a little longer than usual and notice how the two of you adjust to each other. That small, steady tuning is how the old hand keeps writing.
