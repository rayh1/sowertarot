## The card

An old keeper stands in the arched doorway of a small white shrine. He faces us and lifts a small flame on a short handle. Warm gold light fills the room behind him. Worn steps lead down to where we stand, and dark cypresses keep watch on either side.

Everything in the picture points inward, toward rest. Only the keeper stays on the threshold, holding up the light for whoever comes.

## Essence

The Sanctuary is the place set apart where a tired person is let in and restored. It stands for the quiet core of the self that the world's demands cannot reach, and for the choice to step back for a while so you can return with strength. Reversed, that refuge is blocked from the inside. You keep the door open for everyone and never walk through it, or you guard the place so closely that it stops being a rest and becomes one more duty.

## For today

Look at the keeper. He welcomes every traveller, but he never sits down in the warm room behind him. If you have been the steady one for others lately, the listener, the one who always says "come in", notice how long you have stood in that doorway. The welcome is real and good. It is still something you do, and it is yours to pick up and to put down.

Whose rest are you keeping the light up for, and when did you last climb the steps as a guest?

Today, hold your keeper's job out in front of you and look at it plainly. Then choose it on purpose, or lower the flame for an hour and let someone else hold the door. Go into the quiet room as the traveller, not the host. The light will still be there when you come back out.
