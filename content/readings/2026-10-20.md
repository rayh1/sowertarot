## The card

A young traveller stands on a rocky summit in a grey hooded cloak, an open brass compass on his palm. Far below, a broad river winds between steep ridges. It never runs straight for long. It turns where the mountains turn, and it keeps moving toward the sea on the horizon.

Above him one bright star shines in a deepening blue sky. Gold still lights the clouds at the edge of the world. From up here he can see the whole course of the river at once: every bend, and where the bends lead.

## Essence

The Compass Rose is the sense of direction you carry inside you. It does not need the whole road in view. It needs one fixed point and a steady hand. Seen from the summit, the river shows what that looks like in practice: a course that gives way to the land at every turn and never loses where it is going.

## For today

Where is your plan bending today, and are you treating the bend as a failure? The river does not argue with the ridge. It goes around and keeps its heading. A detour is not a change of destination.

Pick one thing today that did not go the straight way you planned. Name where it was meant to lead. Then take the next step around the obstacle, not through it.

Check your compass, not your map. If the direction still holds, the extra turns are just the shape of the land.
