## The card

A young traveller in a grey hooded cloak and tall leather boots stands on a rocky summit. He holds a brass compass on his open palm, its lid up, the needle free to swing. He is not clutching it or hiding it in a pocket. Anyone beside him could lean in and read it.

Below him a river winds between steep ridges toward a distant sea. Above, one star outshines all the rest in a deep teal-green sky, and the clouds along the horizon glow gold. It is twilight, and he looks sure of his way.

## Essence

The Compass Rose is the sense of direction you carry inside you: the quiet knowing that tells you which way is true, even when the road is hidden. It gives you a heading, never a full route, and it works best when you stand still and steady enough to read it. Upright, the card says this faculty is working. You know more about where you are going than you think.

## For today

Who walks this stretch with you? A colleague on a shared project, a partner, a friend weighing the same choice you are. Each of you holds a needle, and each needle settles a little differently.

Today, open your hand. Tell one of these people which way your compass points on something you both face, and why it settles there. Then ask to see theirs. Do not argue the two headings into one. Lay them side by side and look at where they agree and where they part.

A single reading is a guess. Two readings taken from different spots on the same summit start to show where north really lies. What you know grows clearer when it is shared, and theirs does too.
