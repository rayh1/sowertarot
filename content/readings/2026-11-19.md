## The card

A pale green chrysalis hangs from a low, bare branch of an old tree. It does not rest on the wood. It dangles on a long silken thread, one fine line between the branch and everything happening inside the green case. The thread does nothing showy. It holds, and it stays still.

Below, a girl in a plain linen dress kneels in the grass and looks up. Brown leaves drift past her, and a spray of pink blossom hangs beside the chrysalis. The light is the soft peach of dawn, and nothing in the scene moves fast.

## Essence

The Chrysalis is change from the inside: what went in comes apart so that something new can form. It looks like rest, yet it is hard, hidden work. It cannot be hurried or watched. What it needs from outside is small and plain: a fixed point to hang from, and quiet. Upright, the change is on course, as long as that fixed point holds.

## For today

Something in your life is in the middle of a change: a new role, an old belief coming loose, a way of living that no longer fits. What is your thread? It might be a morning walk, a friend you call each week, the hour you go to bed. Small, plain and fixed.

Keep it still today. Don't tug on it, and don't swap it for something new because everything else is shifting anyway. Above all, don't hang a second change from the same branch. Say no to the fresh plan, the new habit or the sudden decision that would add weight while the first change is still forming.

A thread holds best when nobody pulls on it. Let yours be the cool, steady line in a day that moves around it.
