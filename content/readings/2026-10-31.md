## The card

A woman in blue stands on a low ledge in a deep canyon. She has just called out. Now her hands rest at her lips, and she is waiting in the hush that follows. Red, ochre and violet walls rise on both sides of her, ready to send her voice back.

Below her a green river runs toward a small sun at the canyon's end. Above, a larger sun wears rings of light, and swallows cross between the cliffs. The evening is golden and still. Everything in the picture leans toward one moment: the answer that has not come yet.

## Essence

The Echo is the card of call and response. What you send out comes back, shaped by the walls it struck on the way. Reversed, the call keeps going but the listening stops. The voice fills the canyon so fully that no answer can find a gap to return through. Or the hands stay at the lips too long, and the call is never made at all. Either way, the pause where meaning forms has gone missing.

## For today

Look at her hands. They are not shaping another shout. They simply rest there, holding the silence open. Ask yourself where you have been calling without that rest: the email sent three times, the point made again in a meeting, the question asked before the last answer arrived.

Today, make one call and then stop. Put your hands down, feel your feet on the floor, and give the reply time to cross the canyon. Count the quiet in seconds if that helps. What comes back may sound different from what you sent, and that difference is the message.
