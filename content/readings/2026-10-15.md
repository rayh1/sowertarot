## The card

Look below the ground line. Most of the eye goes to the golden taproot, but around it the oak's roots run out sideways through the dark soil, as wide as the branches above. Each of those side roots is thin. None of them glows. Together they hold the tree upright and bring in most of what it drinks.

Above ground the oak is bare, and a young woman kneels in the grass with her hand stretched toward the trunk. The only warm light in the scene comes from under the earth.

## Essence

The Root stands for the hidden base that every visible thing grows from. Its strength is not one deep anchor but a whole system, deep and wide at once, reaching into what is real and lasting. Reversed, that base has narrowed. Everything rests on one strong root, one source, one habit, one person, while the others thin out from neglect. The tree still stands, but it is holding on by a single line.

## For today

Ask yourself which of your roots have gone quiet because the main one seemed to be enough. It might be a friend you only hear from at birthdays, a skill you stopped practising, a place you used to walk, a part of your family story you never asked about.

Pick one of those thin roots and give it a small, real pull today. Send the message. Open the old notebook. Take the long way home past the place. It will not glow, and it will not feel important.

A tree that stands on one root falls in the first storm. One that keeps many small roots alive barely notices the wind.
