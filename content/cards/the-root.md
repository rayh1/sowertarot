# The Root

*An original tarot card in the Rider-Waite tradition*

![The Root](/media/card_the-root.png)

## Visual Description

### Composition

A great old oak dominates the upper half of the card, its bare branches spreading across a deep blue twilight sky. Beside the trunk a young woman in a plain white linen dress and leather boots kneels on the grass, one hand reaching toward the tree. At the ground line the card is cut away like a cross-section: below it, the oak's roots spread wide through dark brown soil, and at the center one thick taproot runs straight down, glowing gold from within, fanning out where it meets a layer of grey bedrock at the bottom.

### Symbolism

The cut-away earth shows what normally stays hidden: the root system is as large as the crown it feeds. The glowing taproot is the living foundation — the source of strength, reaching all the way to bedrock, where it spreads rather than stops. The kneeling woman is the one who remembers to honour what she cannot see; her reaching hand is a gesture of gratitude and listening, not of taking. The bare branches mark a season of rest, when all the tree's life is gathered below ground.

### Atmosphere

Twilight, with a single pale moon and a few early stars. Still, quiet and reverent. The only warm light in the scene rises from beneath the earth.

## Archetypal Essence

The Root represents the profound truth that all visible growth depends on invisible foundations. It speaks to the unglamorous, patient work of establishing deep connections—to the earth, to our past, to the fundamental truths that sustain us. This card reminds us that strength comes not from reaching toward the sky alone, but from how deeply and widely we anchor ourselves in what is real, enduring, and nourishing. The Root is the silent partner to every flowering, the hidden network that makes all manifestation possible.
